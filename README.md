## Key Features

* **Zero-Download Rolling Buffer:** Monitors live streams and long replays (10+ hours) in a lightweight rolling buffer without downloading huge files to disk.
* **Smart Hype Detection:** Auto-clips on audio volume spikes, live/replay **Chat WPM (Words Per Minute)** surges, and hype keywords—with **Auto-Track** & 1-click **Auto-Set (8s Scan)** to match any stream automatically.
* **Instant Manual Clipping:** Hit **⚡ Instant Clip** anytime to save the last 20–25 seconds immediately.
* **Pro Vertical Layouts:** Top 1/3 facecam + bottom 2/3 **Center Lock (Steady Gaming)** or **Smooth AI Follow (Gimbal)**, plus a 1-click **Full-Screen (No Facecam)** mode.
* **Smart Silence & Freeze Chopper:** Automatically jump-cuts silent dead air and frozen frames before captioning so clips stay punchy and tight.
* **Word-Level Yellow/White Captions:** Local Whisper AI karaoke captions with **Auto-Detect, English, French, Spanish, and Arabic** support and studio **48kHz stereo** audio.

## 60-Second Quick Start

1. **Paste a Link:** Drop any Twitch live/VOD, YouTube video, Kick link, or local `.mp4` path into **Stream or Video Link** on the Dashboard.
2. **Select Facecam (Optional):** Click **Select Facecam**, drag a box around the streamer's camera (and jump to any minute on long replays), then click **Save**. *(Or turn off **Top 1/3 Facecam Split** in Settings for full-screen gameplay.)*
3. **Start Clipping:**
   * Click **Start Auto-Clipper** to automatically capture hype moments hands-free.
   * Click **⚡ Instant Clip** anytime to grab a clip immediately.
4. **Find Your Clips:** Click **Open Clips Folder** in the sidebar—all finished vertical `.mp4` clips are saved to `Desktop\WhipClip_Clips`.

## Recommended Settings

Open **Settings** in the left sidebar to fine-tune your clips (changes save automatically):

| Setting | Recommended | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Auto-Track Volume & Chat WPM** | **ON** | Automatically keeps triggers slightly above the stream's average volume & chat speed. |
| **Clip Length** | **20–25s** | Ideal pacing for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. |
| **After-Spike** | **5–6s** | Captures the full reaction after the hype moment hits. |
| **Cooldown** | **30–40s** | Prevents overlapping duplicate clips of the same moment. |
| **Cut Silence & Freezes** | **ON** | Automatically trims dead air and frozen frames before captioning. |
| **Gameplay Framing** | **Center Lock** | Rock-solid steady crop for gaming; use **Smooth AI Follow** for IRL/podcasts. |
| **Words / Line** | **3–4** | Keeps big yellow/white captions punchy on one line. |

## Quick Tips & Troubleshooting

* **Light / Dark Theme:** Click the **☀ / ☾** button at the top of the sidebar to switch between Apple-style Light and Dark themes.
* **Alerts & Status:** Click **▸ Activity & Alerts** at the bottom of the Dashboard to view clip export confirmations or quick error messages.
* **Low-End Laptops (8 GB RAM / CPU-Only):** In **Settings → Layout & Captions**, set AI Caption Quality to **Fast (Low-End Laptop)** for ~1.5s captions without a dedicated GPU.
